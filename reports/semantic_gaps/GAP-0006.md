# GAP-0006 — Calculus rule execution and derivative evaluation

## Exact mathematical reasoning requirement

For `f(x) = 1/x`, enforce `x != 0`, derive `f'(x) = -1/x^2`, and evaluate at `x = 2` to
obtain `f'(2) = -1/4`.

## Trajectories/examples

- G10, actions `a1`–`a2`.

## Current HML constructs inspected

- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/parser/__init__.py`: `derivative(body, x : T)`.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/ast/nodes.py`: `DerivativeExpr` and binder handling.
- `hml_core/examples/analysis/derivative_and_integral.hml`: derivative expressions are
  represented and dimension-checked, while the example explicitly says they are not
  evaluated.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/reasoning/partiality.py`: definedness obligations for
  partial arithmetic terms.

## Why they are insufficient

The current HML derivative construct records a mathematical object and checks its type and
dimension, but it does not apply differentiation rules or evaluate the resulting expression
at a point. The nonzero precondition is representable; the calculus computation is not
verified by the HML compiler.

## Classification

`EXPRESSIBLE_WITH_LIBRARY` — a calculus/symbolic-algebra library could provide the derivative
and exact rational evaluation while preserving the current HML surface. No HML change is
implemented here.
