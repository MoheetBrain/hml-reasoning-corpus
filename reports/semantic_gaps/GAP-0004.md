# GAP-0004 — Quantified theorem discharge

## Exact mathematical reasoning requirement

Bind `n : Natural`, preserve the binder scope, apply the additive identity law to the
arbitrary bound variable, and discharge `forall n : Natural . n + 0 = n`.

## Trajectories/examples

- G07, action `a1`.

## Current HML constructs inspected

- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/parser/__init__.py`: typed quantifier parsing.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/ast/nodes.py`: `Quantifier` and binder identity.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/reasoning/kernel.py`: the closed `RULES` table has no
  quantified identity/generalization rule.
- `hml_core/tests/semantics/test_v2_expressions.py`: quantifier scope and alpha-equivalence
  are tested as representation properties.

## Why they are insufficient

Current HML faithfully represents the binder and its proposition, but its reasoning kernel
does not execute a theorem under a binder or certify a universally quantified goal. The
rendering therefore parses with an open goal while the canonical trajectory records a
separately verified symbolic identity.

## Classification

`REQUIRES_NEW_CORE_SEMANTIC` — quantified inference/generalization must become a trusted
reasoning operation if HML is to certify this step. No implementation is proposed here.
