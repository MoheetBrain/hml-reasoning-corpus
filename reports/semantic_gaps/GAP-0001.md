# GAP-0001 — Arithmetic normalization and numeric evaluation

## Exact mathematical reasoning requirement

After a checked equality-preserving transformation, normalize expressions such as
`x - 0 = 5 - 2` to `x = 3`, or evaluate `0.5 * 0.8` to `0.4`.

## Trajectories/examples

- G01, action `a2`: isolate `x` after subtracting 2.
- G02, action `a1`: evaluate `2 + 3`.
- G09, action `a1`: evaluate the probability product.
- G10, action `a2`: simplify `-1 / (2^2)`.

## Current HML constructs inspected

- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/parser/__init__.py`: additive and multiplicative expression parsing.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/reasoning/kernel.py`: structural equality and the
  equality/inequality transformation rules.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/backends/canonical.py`: canonical identity, which is
  structural rather than a general arithmetic normalizer.

## Why they are insufficient

The current kernel checks that a claimed conclusion is the exact structural output of a
rule. It does not simplify constants, cancel `x - 0`, reorder multiplication, or evaluate
closed numeric expressions. A separate exact arithmetic library can verify these steps, but
the HML rendering cannot carry the final normalized fact as a checked derivation without
adding an unrecorded computation step.

## Classification

`EXPRESSIBLE_WITH_LIBRARY` — this looks like library functionality, not a new HML core
semantic. The corpus uses an exact integer/decimal/rational oracle and does not patch HML.
