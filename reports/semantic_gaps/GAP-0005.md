# GAP-0005 — Conditional-probability product inference

## Exact mathematical reasoning requirement

From `P(A) = 0.5` and `P(B | A) = 0.8`, apply the conditional-probability identity
`P(A and B) = P(A) P(B | A)`.

## Trajectories/examples

- G09, action `a0`.

## Current HML constructs inspected

- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/parser/__init__.py`: `probability(event | condition)`.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/ast/nodes.py`: `ProbabilityExpr` and event structure.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/type_system/__init__.py`: probability/event typing.
- `hml_core/examples/statistics/conditional_expectation.hml`: conditional probability is
  represented and type-checked as a probability-valued expression.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/reasoning/kernel.py`: no probability product rule.

## Why they are insufficient

HML can state the marginal and conditional quantities and can represent the joint event,
but no current rule connects them. The type checker verifies kinds and domains; it does not
apply the probability identity or carry the resulting equality as a checked derivation.

## Classification

`REQUIRES_NEW_CORE_SEMANTIC` — the conditional product rule and its definedness conditions
would need a trusted reasoning rule, not a new spelling for an existing construct. No
implementation is proposed here.
