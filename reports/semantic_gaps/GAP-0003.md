# GAP-0003 — First-class undetermined epistemic result

## Exact mathematical reasoning requirement

Given `P -> Q` and `Q`, report that `P` is undetermined: the premises do not entail `P`,
but they also do not entail `not P`.

## Trajectories/examples

- G06, actions `a0`–`a1`.

## Current HML constructs inspected

- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/ast/nodes.py`: `GoalStatus` contains `OPEN`, `PROVED`,
  `REFUTED`, and `UNSUBSTANTIATED`, but no `UNDETERMINED` outcome.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/reasoning/check.py`: goal adjudication requires a
  verified derivation and otherwise leaves the goal open/unsubstantiated.
- `hml_core/src/hamiltonian_lang/reasoning/kernel.py`: modus ponens and modus tollens are
  available, but there is no non-entailment/countermodel rule.

## Why they are insufficient

An HML `goal` can state `P`, but a clean HML result cannot distinguish “not proved yet” from
“proved to be undetermined.” The countermodel is canonical mathematical evidence, not merely
an absent proof, and its epistemic status must survive into the state.

## Classification

`REQUIRES_NEW_CORE_SEMANTIC` — this is a missing epistemic outcome and countermodel
adjudication semantic, not syntax sugar. No implementation is proposed here.
